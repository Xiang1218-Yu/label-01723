The Classic Game, Reimagined

Tetris has been captivating players since 1984, and for good reason. There's something deeply satisfying about watching those blocks fall into place, clearing lines, and chasing that next high score. It's a game that's easy to learn but impossible to master.

We created Tetris Online because we believe everyone deserves access to this timeless classic without barriers. No app store downloads, no account creation, no subscription fees. Just open your browser and play.

Our version is built from the ground up for mobile devices. We've carefully designed the controls to feel natural on a touchscreen, with swipe gestures for movement and tap-to-rotate functionality. The game board is sized perfectly for phone screens, and the colorful blocks are easy to see even on smaller displays.

Whether you're a Tetris veteran looking for a quick game during your commute or a newcomer discovering the joy of block-stacking for the first time, we've got you covered. So what are you waiting for? Those blocks aren't going to stack themselves!

How to Play

1

Move the Blocks

Swipe left or right to move falling pieces. Use the on-screen buttons for precise control.

2

Rotate to Fit

Tap the screen or press the rotate button to spin pieces and find the perfect fit.

3

Clear Lines

Complete horizontal lines to clear them and earn points. Clear multiple lines at once for bonus scores!

4

Level Up

As you clear more lines, the game speeds up. How long can you survive?
