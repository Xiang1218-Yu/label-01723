Introduction

Welcome to Tetris Online ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we handle information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, use, and disclosure when you use our website and services.

This policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and other applicable privacy laws.

We want to be clear about what we don't do:

  • We do NOT require account registration or login

  • We do NOT knowingly collect information from children under 13

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Tetris Online is designed to be a general audience website. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Our COPPA Commitments:

  • We do NOT knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age
  • We do NOT require any personal information to play our game
  • We do NOT have user accounts, chat features, or social features that could collect children's data
  • Our analytics service (Plausible) does not collect personal information from any users
  • We do NOT use behavioral advertising or targeted advertising

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately at contact@tetris-online.com. We will promptly delete any such information.

  • Right to Correct: You can request correction of inaccurate personal information

European Union Residents' Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

Your Rights

  • Right of Access: Request access to your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request limitation of data processing
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time (where applicable)
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with a supervisory authority

Legal Basis for Processing

We process the limited data we collect based on:

  • Legitimate Interests: To operate, secure, and improve our website
  • Legal Obligations: To comply with applicable laws
